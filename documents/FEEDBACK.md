### DEBE Feedback Types

## Difficult 
- Lacks explanation/examples
- I don’t know enough to understand
- Not enough background provided
- Unclear or cluttered presentation
- Too fast
- Language barrier

## Easy 
- Good explanation 
- I already know this concept
- Easy concept
- The teacher makes it sound easy

## Boring 
- Too difficult content
- Too easy for me
- Content is boring
- Content is not meaningful to me
- Presentation style
- Too slow or repetitive teaching

## Engaging
- Great examples/case study
- Great explanation of a concept
- I already know this concept
- Interesting topic
- Intellectually challenging
